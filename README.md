Turn Muse into the backstage operator for four money-making channels at once. Each skill teaches Muse one consistent, field-tested operating procedure for a platform — planning, scripts, prompts, packaging and analytics — while you keep the accounts, film/render and publish.

## The 4 skills

- **muse-youtube-shorts** (YouTube Shorts) — channel positioning and the two operating modes, the 3-layer hook, escalation scripts and loop design, per-scene AI video prompts, packaging for all 3 distribution surfaces, the Shorts → long-form bridge, and Analytics diagnosis by VVSA and retention-curve shape.
- **muse-facebook-kiem-tien** (Facebook) — a monthly content plan on an entertainment / tips / selling ratio, muted-first Reels scripts, text and sales posts, Amazon affiliate packaging done right from day one, clean cross-posting rules, and weekly Business Suite diagnosis.
- **muse-tiktok-kiem-tien** (TikTok) — niche research and series-based ideation, second-by-second scripts, 10 hook formulas, the 48–72 hour trend window with a trend-fit rubric, the full caption/hashtag/text package, cross-posting, affiliate review scripts, and completion-rate benchmarks.
- **muse-x-kiem-tien** (X / Twitter) — a daily news scan cross-checked against at least two independent sources, single posts written to the X standard (first-line hook, max 280 characters, source link in the first reply), 5–7 post threads, 16:9 editorial images, quality-reply engagement, and a weekly report.

## How to use this preview

Copy a skill folder into your Muse custom-skills directory (or paste the SKILL.md into another AI assistant's chat or project instructions) and ask Muse to run that platform's workflow with you. The lite editions give Muse the principles and the shape of each procedure — enough to plan a channel and draft content in the right structure.
