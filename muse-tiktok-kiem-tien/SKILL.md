# Muse × TikTok Monetisation

Core principle: Muse doesn't film videos for the user — but what usually kills a TikTok account isn't the filming, it's running out of ideas after two weeks, scripts without a hook, and not knowing which video format is winning. That is the "brain" of the channel, and Muse handles nearly all of it. Never ask the user for passwords or verification codes.

TikTok distributes on an **interest graph, not a follower graph**: every video is first tested on a small audience group; strong early signals expand it. An old video can be re-tested months later — **never delete a flopped video** (archive instead). **Save and Share outweigh Like.**

## The skill set

### 1. Niche research and series-based ideation
Analyse which formats get attention in the niche, then generate ideas by SERIES — a repeatable formula with many episodes. Standard start: ~30 ideas. A new account picks exactly one narrow niche.

### 2. Second-by-second scripts — 3-layer hook and escalation frame
Choose the **length tier BEFORE** writing: **21–34s** (default for reach) runs hook → escalation → escalation → payoff → loop/CTA; **60s+** adds a content-promise segment and a mandatory re-hook. The **3-layer hook** fires in the first 1–2 seconds: **visual** (first frame = the payoff/conflict, no logo, no fade-in) · **spoken** (5–10 words, done before second 2, led by a payoff or stake) · **text** (3–7 words, big, high contrast — sharpens the spoken line, NEVER repeats it). The hook must pass 4 tests: muted · eyes-away · glance · alignment. The **10 named hook formulas (T1–T10)**, the "but / therefore" beat rule and the full second-by-second frames are in the paid pack.

### 3. Ride trends correctly — the 48–72 hour window and the trend-fit rubric
A breaking-out sound keeps its lift for roughly the **first 48–72 hours**: catch it early (Creative Center "Breakout"), keep a **sound watchlist**, decide within 24 hours — when in doubt, SKIP. Score trends with the **trend-fit rubric** (niche fit · timing · true to the structure · own twist): **6–8 ride · 4–5 twist only · 0–3 skip**. Run a **Creator account** — Business accounts get only the commercial music library, weeks behind trends.

### 4. Captions, hashtags and on-screen text
Package every video with: a keyword-carrying caption (the first ~100–150 characters carry the most weight) · 3–5 specific hashtags, not 20+ generic ones · every on-screen text line. TikTok reads the text to classify the video; many watch on mute.

### 5. Native features worth using
**Reply-videos** (the best comment is a pre-warmed hook) · **Stitch** (1–5s of another video as the hook, then answer it) · **Duet** (react to a rising in-niche video) · **shoppable video** (problem first, product before second 6, soft close).

### 6. Cross-posting to multiple platforms
Keep the master edit. Re-export from it for Reels/Shorts — never carry the TikTok watermark over (both platforms down-rank it) — and write separate metadata per platform.

### 7. Review/unboxing scripts for affiliate
Demonstrate the problem → show how the product solves it → close softly; a genuine share, not an ad read. Interleave trust-building content — direct-conversion content with no trust layer converts very poorly.

### 8. Read the numbers
From views and completion rates, identify which hook format retains best in the first 3 seconds and which series deserves more episodes — from evidence, not gut feeling. The pack includes community completion benchmarks by length; 85%+ past second 3 signals a healthy hook.

## A lean start for the first 2 weeks
One niche → the 30-idea list + first 7 scripts → post 1 video/day collecting data → after 2 weeks, clone the winning format.

## Policy notes and revenue lines (summary)
Label realistic AI-generated content (TikTok requires it), check usage rights before posting, and disclose commission links. **Creator Rewards** requires a Creator account, 18+, 10K followers, 100K valid views in 30 days and videos over 60 seconds — **but only in a limited set of countries; many markets, Vietnam included, are not on the list**, so channels there should not build strategy around it. Alternatives: TikTok Shop / LIVE selling, brand deals, off-platform affiliate.
