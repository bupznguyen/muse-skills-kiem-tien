# Muse × X (Twitter) Monetisation

Core principle: content on X is mostly text and images — exactly the two things AI does fastest. Muse scans the news, drafts posts and threads with images; the user reviews quickly, then publishes and engages. One hard rule: **unverified news does not get posted** — one wrong story destroys an account's credibility faster than ten correct ones build it. Never ask the user for passwords or verification codes.

## The daily workflow

### 1. Morning news scan
Sweep the field's sources, filter for post-worthy stories, and cross-check every important story against at least two independent sources. Present each with its reason + source link.

### 2. Draft posts and threads
Single-post standard: **hook in the very first line** (specific, factual, curiosity gap) using the pack's proven opening formulas · **max 280 characters** · **source link in the first reply**, never in the body · 0 hashtags by default · every claim checkable. Threads: 5–7 posts — first = hook + promise, one idea per post, last = summary + follow ask.

### 3. Editorial images
One 16:9 editorial image per post when it helps explain the story. Never use an AI image as documentary imagery of a real event; label AI content where required.

### 4. Post on schedule
Post around your audience's active hours, adjusted from the account's own numbers; space batch posts ~20–30 minutes apart. Correct errors promptly, but avoid editing a post in its first 30 minutes.

### 5. Engagement (the strongest growth signal on X)
Daily quality replies under niche posts — add a fact or a new angle, never spam. Reply to comments in the first hour; ~1 quote-post/day; write posts that invite replies and bookmarks.

### 6. Weekly report
Summarise which posts earned views and bookmarks, which topics pulled followers, and next week's proposed focus — from real numbers.
